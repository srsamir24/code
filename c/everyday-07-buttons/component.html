Buttons

Which one would you use?

1 Shrink to tick

It closes to a circle, spins, then ticks.

2 Progress fill

The label changes colour at the edge of the fill.

3 Press and release

It sinks as you press and springs up as you let go.

4 Slide over

The label moves aside for a spinner.

5 Shake on fail

It nudges side to side and says what went wrong.

6 Burst

Dots fly out the moment it succeeds.

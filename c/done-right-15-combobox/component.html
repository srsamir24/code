Combobox, done right

It suggests, it never decides. What you type stays yours until you pick, arrows and Enter work, Esc backs out, and a screen reader hears how many matches there are.

    Type a city, or press ↓ to see all.

    Type "ber": three suggestions, nothing chosen. ↓ ↑ to move, Enter to pick, Esc to close, Esc again to clear.
    Try "reykjavik" without the accent.

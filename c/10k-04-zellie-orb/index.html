Zellie Orb. The prompts.

The prompts behind a speaker site: fabric bands that open like an accordion, a stereo pair with a live interference pattern, and a sound field that follows your cursor. Run them in order, each one builds on the file from the last.

Open the live site View the full code

You need a 3D speaker as model.glb. We made ours with a text-to-3D generator, then shrank it with gltf-transform.

013D hero

Build a light single-page product site in one HTML file with three.js 0.170 from a CDN. Load model.glb (a round speaker), centre and scale it to fit any screen, with one giant number under it and thin concentric rings behind it. The speaker tilts toward the cursor. One font only: Inter Tight.

02Fabric bands

Add a section of four stacked full-width fabric bands with a woven texture, all in one quiet colour family. The open band grows to hold the speaker, recoloured by one shader uniform that touches only the knit, never the metal. The others fold into named strips. Scrolling walks through them, clicking or arrow keys jump. Fade the outgoing name before the band moves.

03Stereo pair

Add a section where a second speaker rolls in and the two stop side by side. Behind them draw a live halftone of the interference pattern between two sources. The cursor is your seat and the ridge passes through it. Show the delay from each speaker in ms and the dB lost at your seat. An Unlink button makes the ridge leave your seat, Link brings it back.

04Sound field

Add a dark section with contour lines as sound waves around the speaker and three objects: sofa, column, shelf. The speaker follows the cursor and the waves bend around the objects. Keep the lines out from behind the text.

Honest take: this is a beautiful site, and for most businesses it's overkill. Your bakery doesn't need an interference pattern. Use the parts, not the whole thing.
